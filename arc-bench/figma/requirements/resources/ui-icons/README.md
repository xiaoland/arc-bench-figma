# Shared UI icons

The unified [icon library](../icons/README.md) identifies these glyphs by control and state. The glyph box and the button hit area are separate.

| Resource | Role | Native box |
|---|---|---|
| [ChevronDown16](ChevronDown16.svg) | File/Zoom dropdown and expanded section | 16 × 16 |
| [ChevronRight16](ChevronRight16.svg) | Collapsed section or layer subtree | 16 × 16 |
| [SubmenuArrow24](SubmenuArrow24.svg) | Main menu submenu arrow | 24 × 24 |
| [Clear16](Clear16.svg) | Clear a search field | 16 × 16 |
| [More24](More24.svg) | Selected-object actions menu | 24 × 24 |
| [CollapseLayers24](CollapseLayers24.svg) | Collapse expanded layer subtrees | 24 × 24 |
| [Search24](Search24.svg) | Assets search prefix | 24 × 24 |

Close24 and Add24 reuse the verified Effects and Paint assets through the unified registry.
