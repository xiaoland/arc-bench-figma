# Paint icon resources

These reusable SVG references come from the Figma Design Fill, gradient, and Selection colors controls observed on 29 October and 1 November 2026. Names are local resource identifiers. The SVGs inherit currentColor from the control. The table gives the complete SVG display box in CSS pixels. Its internal mark is smaller. Button hit areas are separate.

| Resource | Purpose | CSS display size |
|---|---|---|
| [PaintAdd](PaintAdd.svg) | Add fill | 24 × 24 |
| [ApplyStyles](ApplyStyles.svg) | Apply styles | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintVisible](PaintVisible.svg) | Visible paint | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintHidden](PaintHidden.svg) | Hidden paint | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintRemove](PaintRemove.svg) | Remove paint | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintReorder](PaintReorder.svg) | Reorder paint | 16 × 32 |
| [GradientFlip](GradientFlip.svg) | Flip gradient | 24 × 24 |
| [GradientRotate](GradientRotate.svg) | Rotate gradient | 24 × 24 |
| [SelectionUses](SelectionUses.svg) | Select matching paint users | 24 × 24 |
| [DetachStyle](DetachStyle.svg) | Detach the selected Style entry | 24 × 24 |

| [EditStyle](EditStyle.svg) | Edit the hovered Style | 12 × 14 |
| [ReapplyStyle](ReapplyStyle.svg) | Restore the selected Text Style formatting | 24 × 24 |

| [PaintTypeSolid](PaintTypeSolid.svg) | Solid paint type | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintTypeGradient](PaintTypeGradient.svg) | Gradient paint type | 24 × 24 |
| [PaintTypeImage](PaintTypeImage.svg) | Image paint type | 24 × 24 |

Solid keeps its tertiary mark in `--paint-icon-tertiary`, with the observed light-interface fallback rgba(0,0,0,0.3).
